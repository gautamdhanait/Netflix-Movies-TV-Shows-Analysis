NETFLIX BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The catalog has 8,794 titles from 122 countries. Movies are 70% of the content. United States is the largest producer and International Movies is the most common genre. Releases peaked in 2018 and additions peaked in 2019. About 46% of titles are adult rated. Netflix should keep growing TV series, add family content and widen its country coverage.

KEY INSIGHTS
- Catalog size: 8,794 titles, 6,129 movies (69.7%) and 2,665 TV shows (30.3%).
- Releases peaked in 2018; Netflix added the most titles in 2019.
- Top genres: International Movies, Dramas, Comedies. The catalog is concentrated in a few genres (42 in total).
- United States leads with 3,683 titles; the top 3 countries cover 69% of titles with a known country (122 countries in total).
- Adult ratings (TV-MA, R, NC-17) cover 46% of titles, so family and kids content is the thinner segment.
- The average movie runs 100 min; the average TV show has 1.8 seasons and 67% have only 1 season.
- The top director, Rajiv Chilaka, has only 22 titles, so the catalog does not depend on a few directors.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Keep investing in TV series and recent releases.
- Add more family and kids content to reach households with children.
- Grow content from smaller regions to reduce dependence on a few countries.
- Track the genre mix every year to spot saturation before buying more of the same category.
